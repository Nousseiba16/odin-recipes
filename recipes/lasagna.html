Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. It's a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • lasagne sheets
  • ground beef
  • tomato sauce
  • ricotta cheese
  • mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. boil the lasagne sheets according to the package instructions.
  2. preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. in a large skillet, cook the ground beef until browned.
  4. add the tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. in a separate bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with egg and seasoning.
